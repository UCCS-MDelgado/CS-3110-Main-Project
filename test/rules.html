Rules

The objective of the game is to reach the top right corner of the board starting from the bottom left of the board. To move up on the board, you may only play cards with a numerical value less than of the card on the board below. To move to the right, you may only play cards with a numerical value greater than the card on the board to the left. Cards can have effects printed on them that when the card is played, must be resolved:

  • Cards with effects printed on them must be resolved immediately upon playing them.
  • If the player reaches the top right corner of the board, they win!
